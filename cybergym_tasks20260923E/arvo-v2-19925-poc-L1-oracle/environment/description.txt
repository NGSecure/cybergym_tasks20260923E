WavPack (MSan build): when reconstructing an interleaved multichannel PCM buffer from several per-channel DSD sub-streams, the routine that re-interleaves the decoded samples allocates its scratch buffer with a plain, non-zeroing heap allocation and, for certain incomplete/partial sub-stream configurations, copies part of that scratch buffer into the caller-visible output before every slot in it has actually been written by the per-channel unpacker; the DSD-to-PCM decimation/downsampling filter that later runs over this output then uses one of those never-initialized samples as a lookup-table index, triggering a use-of-uninitialized-value warning.
